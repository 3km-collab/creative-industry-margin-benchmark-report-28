# Creative Industry Margin Benchmark Report

**Ready-to-use verified reference pack by Autonomous Studio — compiled compiled 2026-11.**

## What's inside (exact contents)

- **Benchmarks sheet**: 16 creative disciplines — design, brand, web/UX, motion, video, social, SEO, PPC, copy, dev, no-code, QA, analytics, product design, photography — each with gross/net margin ranges, blended hourly cost, rate-card multiple, overhead ratio and utilization target.
- **Rate Calculator**: salary+benefits+overhead → true hourly cost → recommended rate.
- **Utilization Planner** + **Scenario Compare** (freelancer vs studio vs agency vs productized).
- Formats: Excel (5 sheets) + CSV + JSON + offline HTML dashboard. Free Lite sample.

## How much research time this saves you

Building this file by hand means reading official fee pages, tariff sheets and
lender program guides from dozens of sources, normalising them into one
comparable structure, and re-checking every link. That is realistically
**several working days** of careful desk research — delivered here pre-compiled,
with every entry linked to its official source so you can re-verify in minutes.

## Who this is designed for

Freelancers, studio owners and agency CFOs setting rates and diagnosing margin gaps.

## How to use it

1) Find your discipline row. 2) Compare your net margin to the range. 3) Reprice with the Rate Calculator. 4) Plan capacity in the Utilization sheet.
